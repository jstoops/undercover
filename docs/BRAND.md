# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings and body**: Saira Extra Condensed
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Roboto
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: Courier Prime

## Colors

- **Primary**: Smoke Tree `#bd5d38`, Midnight text
- **Secondary**: Dark grayish blue `#6c757d`, Midnight text
- **Tertiary**: Very dark grayish blue `#343a40`, Midnight text
- **Information**: Pelorous `#17a2b8`, white text
- **Highlight or alert**: Bright red `#dc3545`, white text
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Midnight `#6c757d`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Sea Mist `#ffffff80`

Use Smoke Tree, Dark grayish blue and Very dark grayish blue when three things sit side by side, such as a claim's states or chart series. Add Pelorous fourth and Bright red fifth.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#f8f9fa`, white panels and cards
- **Dark mode**: page background `#343a40`, black panels and cards

Panels are rounded with no outline. Smoke Tree and Dark grayish blue are too bright for small text, so darken them for headings and body text on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
